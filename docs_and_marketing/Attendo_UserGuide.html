ATTENDO

Official User Guide

Welcome to the future of academic attendance. This guide will walk you through the installation, setup, and daily operation of your Attendo software.

Developed by Dr. Belal El-Fakharany

Getting Started

Installation & Activation

01
1

Installation

Double-click the Attendo Setup.exe file provided to you. Follow the standard Windows installation prompts. Once installed, Attendo will launch automatically and place a shortcut on your desktop.

2

Providing Your Hardware ID

Upon first launch, Attendo will display a Machine Hardware ID. This unique signature securely locks the software to your computer. Click the "Copy ID" button and send this securely to your administrator to request a license.

3

Activating Your License

Once you receive your Activation Token (JWT) from the administrator, paste it into the activation box and click Activate License. The software will instantly unlock and display a Welcome confirmation screen with your plan details.

4

Initial Environment Setup

Fill in the required academic details: Instructor Name, University, Faculty, Semester, and Dates. This data is permanently baked into your official PDF and Excel exports. Select the academic grades/years you teach, and assign a Subject Name to each grade.

Daily Operations

Workspaces & Scanning

02

Selecting a Workspace

After setup, you will see your Workspaces (e.g., Grade 1, Grade 2). Click on the class you are currently teaching to enter its dashboard.

PRO TIP: You can customize workspace names (e.g., "Section A") by clicking the Settings gear icon and selecting "Manage Workspaces".

Scanning Student QR Codes

In your workspace, click Start Scanning. Ensure your webcam is connected.

  • Hold the student's ID card up to the camera.
  • The software will instantly beep and log the student.
  • If the student is not registered, the software will prompt you to enter their name to instantly register them on the fly.
  • Toggle "Zen Mode" (Ctrl+D) for a distraction-free scanning interface.

Unified Manual Entry (The Entry Panel)

Don't have a webcam? Click on the Open Entry Panel button in the Scanner dashboard. Here, you can rapidly log students from a paper list, manually type IDs, or paste bulk data from WhatsApp/Excel.

Advanced Features

Exporting & Tools

03

Generating QR Cards

You can generate printable QR code ID cards for your students natively within Attendo.

  1. Go to the Roster tab.
  2. Click the Download QRs button.
  3. Attendo will automatically bundle high-resolution JPG images of the QR codes for the entire class into a ZIP file ready for printing or digital distribution.

Exporting Reports

Attendo generates professional PDF and Excel reports automatically.

Session Export Exports attendance for only the current day's lecture. Found in the scanner dashboard.
Master Report Exports the entire semester's attendance history for all students. Found in the Analytics tab.

Data Synchronization

Attendo runs entirely offline for your privacy. However, you must connect to the internet at least once every 21 days. The software will automatically verify your license status silently in the background. If you exceed 21 days offline, the software will lock itself until an internet connection is provided.
