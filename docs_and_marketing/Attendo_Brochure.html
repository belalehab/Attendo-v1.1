ATTENDO

Focus on Teaching, Attendo Handles the Rest

A secure, intelligent attendance-management ecosystem built specifically for university instructors and teaching assistants.

FOUNDATION & SETUP

Built for Scale and Privacy

Multi-Workspace Architecture

Manage completely isolated databases for different grades, subjects, or courses simultaneously without ever mixing your student rosters or attendance data.

Effortless Spreadsheet Rosters

Onboarding thousands of students takes seconds. Simply drag and drop your university's raw spreadsheets directly into the software, and Attendo flawlessly organizes your rosters automatically.

Complete Customization

Make the reports your own. Upload your custom logos through the interactive settings panel, and they will be beautifully embedded in all your exported documents.

Privacy & Security

Built for absolute privacy. The software runs completely offline—meaning your sensitive student information and attendance records never touch the cloud or leave your secure machine.

System Vault & Continuous Backups

Never lose a single record. Attendo automatically creates continuous, secure backups in the background, ensuring your important academic data is always safe, protected, and recoverable.

EFFORTLESS ATTENDANCE CAPTURE

Built for Speed and Flexibility

Multi-Device Scanning

Scan students using your built-in laptop camera, or effortlessly link multiple mobile phones via hotspot. Any number of Teaching Assistants can connect simultaneously to scan hundreds of students in seconds.

Unified Manual Entry

Forgot a QR code? The intuitive search bar lets you instantly look up and manually register students by name or ID without ever leaving the live session screen.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Designed for power users. Navigate the entire application, trigger exports, toggle the camera, and search for students instantly using a comprehensive suite of keyboard shortcuts.

Cold-Call Randomizer

Elevate classroom engagement. The built-in randomizer intelligently selects only from the pool of currently present students, keeping the academic environment interactive and fair.

ANALYTICS & AUTOMATION

Data-Driven Academic Insights

Comprehensive Session History

Full control over the past. Access the dedicated History tab to review, modify, or seamlessly append late students to any previously recorded academic session.

Detailed Student-Level Timelines

Click on any individual student to view their personal, granular timeline. Track exactly which days they attended and when they were absent across the entire semester.

AI Sparklines

Experience the power of AI. Our smart visual sparklines automatically calculate absence thresholds and flag at-risk students instantly, helping you intervene before they fall behind.

Dual-Format Master Exports

In a single click, Attendo simultaneously generates a beautifully stylized, highly compressed PDF report AND a data-rich Excel workbook. Both files are automatically saved and neatly categorized into organized, dedicated folders directly on your Desktop for effortless record-keeping.

Database Merging & Conflict Resolution

Collaboration made offline. Manually export and share your local databases with other TAs. When importing their records back, Attendo's intelligent Conflict Resolver automatically detects duplicates and seamlessly merges discrepancies to maintain perfect data integrity.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

Optimized for Academic Hardware

Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit)

Fully optimized for modern Windows environments. (Note: Windows 7 and 8 are not supported).

Ultra-Lightweight Footprint

Designed to run flawlessly on standard laptops. Requires only 4GB of RAM (8GB recommended) and less than 150MB of storage space.

100% Offline Capability

Internet is only required for the initial 1-minute installation. Once installed, Attendo runs completely offline. To use mobile phones as scanners, simply activate a local mobile hotspot.

Universal Camera Support

Use any standard built-in laptop webcam, a cheap USB external camera, or seamlessly link your personal smartphone to use its camera wirelessly.
