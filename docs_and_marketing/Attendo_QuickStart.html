Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in 60 seconds.

1

Activate Your License

Launch the Attendo application on your Windows machine. When prompted, paste the JWT Activation Token provided to you via WhatsApp or Email. Once verified, your local database is initialized and securely encrypted.

Note: You only need to do this once. Your license will remain active offline for up to 21 days before requiring a brief internet connection to sync.

2

Scan Student QR Codes

You have two powerful ways to scan attendance at the start of your lecture:

Laptop Camera

Navigate to the "Scanner" tab and hold student IDs up to your webcam.

Mobile Hotspot

Connect your laptop to your phone's hotspot, scan the on-screen QR, and use your phone's camera to walk around the lecture hall.

3

Export Master Reports

At the end of the week or semester, navigate to the "Export" tab. With one click, Attendo will generate comprehensive Excel spreadsheets and PDF documents detailing every student's attendance history.

Files are automatically saved to organized, dedicated folders directly on your computer's Desktop.
