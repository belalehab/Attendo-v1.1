I am thrilled to officially announce the launch of **Attendo**—a project I have been building to solve one of the most frustrating problems in academia: Manual Attendance. For years, I watched colleagues spend 15 minutes of precious lecture time calling out names or passing around paper rosters. Not only does this waste time that should be spent teaching, but it almost always leads to academic fraud (students signing in for absent friends) and ruins weekends spent manually typing paper data into Excel. Cloud-based solutions existed, but they were often expensive subscriptions that required a constant internet connection and raised data-privacy concerns by hosting student records on third-party servers. So, I built something better. 🚀 **Introducing Attendo:** Attendo is an offline, desktop-first attendance tracker built specifically for university professors. ✅ **Lightning Fast QR Scanning:** Students simply show their ID to your laptop webcam (or your phone's camera via a local hotspot). Attendo marks them present in 0.2 seconds. ✅ **100% Offline & Local:** Your student data stays strictly on your hard drive. No cloud servers, no subscription fees, no internet connection required to scan. ✅ **One-Click Exports:** At the end of the semester, generate master Excel and PDF reports natively on your Desktop with a single click. It is time to stop managing papers and start managing success.
