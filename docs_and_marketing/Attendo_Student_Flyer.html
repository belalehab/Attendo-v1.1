Welcome to Class!

We are using Attendo for attendance this semester.

To maximize our lecture time, we are replacing manual roll calls with an instant QR scanning system. Please follow these instructions to ensure you are marked present.

1. Get Your QR Code

Save your official University ID QR code to your phone's photo gallery, or keep your physical ID card handy.

2. Brightness Up

If using your phone, maximize your screen brightness before scanning to ensure the camera reads it instantly.

3. Scan & Go

Hold your QR code up to the professor's laptop or mobile camera. You'll be marked present in 0.2 seconds!

Academic Integrity Notice

Scanning a QR code belonging to an absent student constitutes academic fraud and will be subject to university disciplinary action.
